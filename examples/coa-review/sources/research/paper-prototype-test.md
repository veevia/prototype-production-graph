# Paper-prototype test — results (fictional research for the pdg example)

Five reviewers worked through three paper lots (compliant, out-of-spec, unreadable value) on a printed comparison layout
with a derived verdict.

- 4 of 5 reviewers released the compliant lot from the derived verdict without re-keying any value (R3 re-keyed "out of
  habit").
- All 5 held the out-of-spec lot; 2 of 5 first looked for a "send to supplier" button.
- In the debrief, the QA lead (L1) agreed the lot-release flow and the rule that the tool never releases a lot itself.
