# Retro — &lt;project&gt; — YYYY-MM-DD

> **👥 For the team**
>
> 45–60 minutes. Blameless. Every action item gets an owner and a deadline — or it doesn't count. We review last retro's actions first, because a retro that doesn't close its own loops is theater.

> **TL;DR:** &lt;One sentence — the single biggest takeaway from this cycle and the recommended forward path.&gt;

## Who participates

| Function | What they bring | Time |
|---|---|---|

| Engineering | Technical learnings; what shipped vs. what broke | Full session |
| Research / UX | User feedback synthesis | Full session or async input |
| Marketing / GTM | Field reactions; positioning adjustments | 15 min or async input |
| Executive sponsor | Forward-path decision authority | Last 15 min or async |
