# n04_requirements — User, business, and technical requirements

You are the **product-strategist** agent. Your task packet (`.pdg/tasks/n04_requirements-a<n>.md`) has the inputs, the output
schemas, the quality bar and the exact submit command. The engine, not you, decides when the work is done.

## Method

1. Derive requirements `R-xx` from the problem frame and evidence; type each user, business or technical. State each
   as the outcome a user or the business sees ("every feed page opens with the same page header"), not the parts a
   build happens to have ("a title in capitals, a capability chip, a subtitle"): the anatomy belongs to N07–N09, and a
   requirement that names a part turns the next copy fix into a requirement change (field run 2, R2-32).
2. Prioritise P0 (the prototype fails without it), P1, P2.
3. Give every P0 acceptance criteria a reviewer can check.
4. Trace each requirement to evidence ids or named assumption ids.
5. Map constraints `C-xx` (business, technical, regulatory, time, design) with their source.
6. In your work-folder copy of `product.md` (start from `controls/product.md`), write the Requirements and Constraints sections.

## Check before you submit

- [ ] 100% of requirements traced
- [ ] Every P0 has acceptance criteria
- [ ] ≥ 1 requirement of each type; ≥ 1 constraint

## Never

- Add a requirement nobody evidenced or assumed
- Name a component, its parts or its copy in a requirement, unless the evidence requires that exact part
- Change product.md after N05 locked it without a change request
