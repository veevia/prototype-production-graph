# Design principles library

> **TL;DR:** 77 rules in rule base v1.0.22, each a testable check with its evidence. 63 adopted, 5 candidate, 9 validated. Adopted rules block; validated rules advise until Wei approves them; candidates only inform.

Generated from `knowledge/rules.json` (the governed store) and the de-identified case study. Evidence ids resolve in `knowledge/public/evidence.json`. **Stable** principles apply to every project; **project-specific** preferences never become defaults.

Basis: *canonical-framework* = Wei's canonical frameworks and workspace constitution · *stated-requirement* = Wei's own recorded words · *graduated-skill* = Wei's skill library · *observed-pattern* = a verified record whose author is not identified, or a pattern across records (advisory until approved) · *recommendation* = introduced by this workflow (informational). A seed approval is recorded as `pdg seed` (human: false): it claims no human decision until Wei confirms it (`pdg memory confirm <rule> --by Wei`).
