# Evidence in the public distribution

**TL;DR:** Customer case studies, private citations, feedback and personal review histories are not distributed.

The public rule profile preserves the engine's quality defaults, not private evidence or approvals for your project. Its provenance entries in `knowledge/public/evidence.json` describe recommended checks, not observed customer outcomes. Original evidence is **MISSING** from this distribution by design.

The Lot Release Workbench example and every validation session in its dry run are fictional or simulated. Supply your own authorized evidence under your project's `sources/` directory.
