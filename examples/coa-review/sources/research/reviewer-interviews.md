# Reviewer interviews — synthesis (fictional research for the pdg example)

Six 30-minute contextual interviews at the (fictional) Riverside plant: five QA lot reviewers (R1–R5) and the QA lead (L1).

- 4 of 5 reviewers re-key certificate values from the supplier PDF into a spreadsheet before comparing them with the
  specification. R2: "I type every number twice — once into the sheet, once in my head against the limit."
- Reviewers want the value and its limit side by side before they decide. R4: "I need to see the number next to the limit,
  not scroll between them."
- When a value is unreadable (a smudged scan), reviewers phone the QA lead. Nothing records that escalation. L1: "I only
  hear about unreadable certificates when someone calls me."
- Supplier notices are drafted by the reviewer and sent by the supplier-quality manager, never by the reviewer.
